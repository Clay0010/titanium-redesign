Still have a question? Let’s talk
What is Titanium Training Center?

Titanium began its educational journey in 2017. We offer information technology training in collaboration with instructors holding specialized certifications, from both inside and outside Iraq.

Which courses can I study?

Our training follows two paths: Future Wave for people starting in IT, and advanced courses for professional development. Subjects include networking, cybersecurity, databases, programming and system administration. Contact the team to find out which course fits your experience.

Does Titanium work with international organizations?

Yes. Titanium’s international training partners include EC-Council, PECB and CompTIA, supporting cybersecurity, ISO and compliance, and IT certification training.

Can I learn online or attend in person?

Training is available both in person and online. Titanium offers practical, hands-on learning with support during and after your course. Ask our team about the delivery options for your chosen program.

How do I find course dates and register?

Browse our course catalog or contact the team by phone, WhatsApp or email. We can help you confirm the current course schedule, requirements, fees and availability before you register.
