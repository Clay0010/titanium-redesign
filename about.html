Our story

A journey that
started in 2017.

Titanium began its educational journey in 2017. Since then, the center has brought learners together with instructors holding specialized certifications from inside and outside Iraq.

Our training covers networking, cybersecurity, databases, programming and system administration, with pathways for beginners and professionals developing their skills.
